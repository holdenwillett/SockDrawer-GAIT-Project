Your wardrobe. Your choice.

Wardrobe details and compressed clothing photos are saved in this browser using IndexedDB. They aren’t synced to an account. Browser data clearing removes them.

Photos are sent to OpenAI only when you choose identification or generate a preview and check its permission box. The server forwards the request without saving photos to disk. Generated previews are also analyzed by OpenAI to describe the visible outfit. OpenAI’s own data handling and retention terms apply.

Your personal try-on photo and generated preview are held in this tab’s memory. Closing or reloading the app clears them. When you request narration and allow text processing, only the written description is sent to ElevenLabs for Eleven v3 speech. Provider retention terms apply. Narration audio is kept in tab memory for replay and cleared when its outfit is replaced or the page reloads.
